# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Image Editor welcomes users and contributors of all backgrounds and experience
levels. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment, discrimination,
or intimidation, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic
background.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
other spaces managed by the project, including official representation at events
or in other communities.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the work, explain disagreements, and
  give others room to respond.
- Welcome questions and different perspectives. Help contributors learn without
  belittling their experience, language, or accessibility needs.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries, including requests to stop contact.
- Accept feedback in good faith, acknowledge mistakes, and help repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory abuse, or encouragement of violence.
- Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or sexualized conduct directed at
  participants.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or repeated disruption of project discussions.
- Sharing another person's private information or communications without consent.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith or helps review it.
- Evading moderation or continuing contact after being asked to stop.

## Reporting a concern

You do not need to confront the person involved before seeking help.

- **Abuse on GitHub:** use
  [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam),
  including when a concern involves a maintainer. GitHub reviews these reports
  under its own policies. Do not assume a report to GitHub also reaches project
  maintainers.
- **Project moderation:** this project does not publish a private contact address.
  To request a private conversation, open a
  [GitHub issue](https://github.com/bensitu/image-editor/issues) titled
  **Moderation contact request**. This request is public: include only a request
  for a private channel, with no names, allegations, or evidence. Wait for a
  suitable channel before sharing the report. If making a public request would
  put you at risk, use GitHub's reporting tools for conduct on GitHub.

When a private project channel has been arranged, share what happened, relevant
links and dates, and any immediate concerns about further contact. Share only
information needed to assess the concern. Maintainers will restrict access to
those handling the report, but cannot guarantee absolute confidentiality.

## Enforcement and review

Maintainers consider the context, impact, severity, and repetition of the conduct.
They may request clarification and explain decisions to affected participants
where appropriate, while protecting report details. An involved maintainer should
step aside when an uninvolved reviewer is available; an independent project
reviewer may not always be available.

Actions may include a request to change behavior, a warning, removal of content,
or a temporary or permanent restriction on participation. Serious conduct may
justify immediate restrictions; these actions need not occur in sequence.

A person affected by a project decision may request a review through the project
moderation process above, explaining the decision at issue and any relevant new
information in private. Responses and reviews depend on maintainer availability;
there is no guaranteed response deadline.

This project policy complements the
[GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
For software vulnerabilities, follow [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) instead.
